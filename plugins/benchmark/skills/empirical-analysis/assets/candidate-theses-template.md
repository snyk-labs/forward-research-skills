# ‹Benchmark name›: candidate theses

> Internal. 3 to 6 candidate narratives backed by claim IDs, ranked. They are not mutually exclusive; a publishable framing usually combines one lead with two supporting findings. The human chooses.

## How candidates are scored

Each thesis gets 1 to 5 on four dimensions. How a thesis reflects on any system, including the authors' own, is not a dimension and never breaks a tie. Composite = evidence + novelty + practical + (6 − caveat severity), out of 20. Ties are broken by evidence strength. If two candidates are within one point, call them co-leads.

| Score | Evidence strength | Novelty (vs the prior-art guide, if one exists) | Practical importance | Caveat severity (higher = worse) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Direct counts, robust under every test in the memo | No prior claimant | Changes a decision directly | Key confound cannot be resolved with this data |
| 4 | Direct counts, robust with stated exceptions | Materially extends a prior claim | Changes how results are read or tools configured | Major confound, partly controlled |
| 3 | Consistent pattern; mechanism inferred | Known idea, new decomposition | Informs the research agenda | Moderate; can be disclosed |
| 2 | Descriptive, one confounded comparison, small strata | Replication | Marginal | Minor |
| 1 | Failed disconfirmation | Already established | None | Negligible |

| Rank | Thesis | Evidence | Novelty | Practical | Caveat | Composite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Say whether the ranking follows or departs from any pre-planned research-question order, and why. If the top theses all favour one system, say so, and list the strongest reading that points the other way with its score.

## T‹n›: ‹thesis, one sentence›

- **Claim and evidence:** ‹claim IDs›.
- **Falsifier:** ‹the result that would break it›.
- **Tests run and outcome:** ‹controls, sensitivity, leave-one-out›.
- **Caveats that must travel with it:** ‹from limitations›.
- **Wording ceiling:** ‹strongest verb the evidence supports, for example "associated with", "consistent with"›.

## Not recommended: T‹n› (failed disconfirmation)

Kept for the record with the test it failed.

## Suggested combinations for the human decision

Lead + two supporting findings, and what each combination commits the write-up to, including the results unfavourable to any system that it must still report.
