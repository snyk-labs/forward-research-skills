# Review kit outline (target: 25 to 30 minutes reading, about 15 to 25 pages)

Footer on every page: "Internal review draft, not for distribution · v‹N›".

- **Cover and "New in v‹N›" box**: what changed since the last version, with any corrections called out visibly.
- **Plain abstract**: what we studied (‹systems› on ‹reference set›), what we found in 5 to 8 sentences, what we need from you, how long it takes.
- **One-page overview**: the corpus (counting bases), the systems and configurations, the registered metric, the scoring rule in one paragraph.
- **What we planned versus what the data said**: the original questions, which held, which did not, why the framing changed.
- **Headline chart**: with k/n labels and its mandatory caveat label.
- **Thesis pages**: for each: claim in one sentence, figure, evidence (claim IDs shown small), caveats, a "Why we decided" box (options considered, choice, reason).
- **Claims never to make**: the list from the guideline, so the reviewer can say if they would add to it.
- **Reviewer checks (pinned in context)**: yes/no questions beside the claim they test. Fairness checks first, for every evaluated system (is each, ‹reference tool› included, configured and run as its owner would expect, under the same rules? is each description accurate and equally hedged?). Then correctness checks. Then wording checks.
- **One-page checklist**: all yes/no checks again, with a space for free comments.
- **Appendix**: configurations, versions, prompts, scoring rule detail, reference-set defects.
