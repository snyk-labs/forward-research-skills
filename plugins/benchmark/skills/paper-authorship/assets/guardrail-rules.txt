# Guardrail rules for a benchmark paper. Copy to paper/guardrail-rules.txt, then:
#   1. add one forbid rule per item in the narrative guideline's "claims never to make" list;
#   2. add one require rule per item in the guideline's "Required disclosures" list (the guideline is the single source;
#      the require rules below are the defaults most papers need, and the commented block lists the design-dependent ones);
#   3. uncomment the design-dependent and task-dependent rules that apply, delete the rest.
# Format: severity | kind | regex | reason   (fields separated by ' | ' with spaces; a bare '|' in a regex is alternation)
# Text is matched with comments removed and each paragraph joined onto one line. Tighten any pattern that produces
# false positives on the current draft; keep the reason readable.

# --- statistics
block  | forbid      | \bstatistically (in)?significant\b|\bsignificantly\b|\bsignificant (difference|gain|improvement|effect|result)s?\b | descriptive statistics only; never claim significance
review | forbid      | \bsignificant\b | in a paper that reports p-values descriptively, "significant" reads as a statistical claim; say "large" or give the numbers
block  | forbid      | \bstatistically indistinguishable\b|\bno (real|meaningful) difference\b | "not separated" is not "equal"; give the counts
block  | forbid      | \bwe prove\b|\bproves that\b|\bconclusively\b | empirical results support, they do not prove

# --- design honesty
block  | forbid      | \bpre-?registered\b|\bregistered (rule|metric|setting|policy)\b | say "fixed before analysis" or "primary" unless a public pre-registration exists
review | forbid      | \b(caused|causes|because of|due to|led to|drives|driven by)\b | causal wording needs a control that isolates the factor; otherwise "is consistent with"

# --- marketing language and slogans
block  | forbid      | \b(revolutionary|game[- ]chang\w*|best[- ]in[- ]class|industry[- ]leading|unparalleled|cutting[- ]edge|world[- ]class|next[- ]gen\w*|seamless\w*|supercharg\w*|blazing(ly)?|unmatched (accuracy|performance|coverage|speed|quality))\b | marketing language
block  | forbid      | \b(clearly|vastly|dramatically|massively) (better|superior|outperform\w*)\b | promotional comparative
nit    | forbid      | \b(delve|tapestry|landscape of|pivotal|underscore[sd]?|leverag\w+|holistic|synerg\w+)\b | stock filler; say it plainly

# --- superlatives: correct sometimes, wrong often; verify each against every set it could cover
review | superlative | \b(only|every|all|none|never|always|first|best|worst|unique(ly)?|sole(ly)?)\b | verify against the registry and every subset/supplementary run
review | superlative | \btop[- ]?(two|three|four|five|\d+)\b | verify membership under every set and rule the sentence could cover

# --- placeholders (also run on prompts/*.txt and references.bib by passing them to --tex)
block  | placeholder | \[(date|k|n|version|tbd|todo)\]|\bTODO\b|\bTBD\b|\bFIXME\b|\bXXX\b|\?\?|‹[^›]*› | unfilled placeholder

# --- required disclosures: defaults (each must match at least once outside headings; adapt the patterns to the paper's wording)
block  | require     | conflicts? of interest|competing interests?|(are|is|were|was) employed by|employees? of | authorship and conflict-of-interest disclosure
block  | require     | reference set .{0,80}(created|curated|seeded|construct|built)|provenance of | provenance of the reference set
block  | require     | (data|code)( and (code|data))? (are|is) (publicly )?(available|released)|released under|available at | data and code availability statement
block  | require     | licen[cs]e[ds]? | licence terms of the released code and data
